# SCRIPT 01 — "Mfs who say 'the manga was better' 💀"

**Format:** Vertical Short (9:16) · **Runtime:** 0:38 · **Cast:** 1 performer, 2 characters
**Pillar:** P2 fan-archetype comedy × P1 cosplay swap · **Hooks used:** H3 (in-group callout) + H1 (deferred payoff)
**Shoot cost:** one room, two shirts, one book. Half a day including edit.

---

## Why this concept

It targets the single most universal in-group friction in the anime fandom, so the
comment section writes itself ("bro this is my friend fr"). It needs two characters,
which forces the costume-swap cut that is King Chris's visual signature. And the
turn — the manga snob getting out-nerded — inverts the setup, so the last four
seconds retroactively rewrite the first four. That's the rewatch mechanic.

---

## Characters

**MANGA GUY** — round wire glasses, buttoned-to-the-top shirt, tote bag. Never blinks
during a flex. Speaks in a flat, patient, deeply condescending register. Always holding
a physical volume, thumb wedged in as a bookmark.

**ANIME GUY** — hoodie, hood up, blanket, snack bowl. Loud, joyful, entirely
uncritical. Watches with his whole body. This is the audience surrogate — the viewer
must like him.

## Edit notes

- **Cut on the costume change, never around it.** Both characters sit in the same
  spot on the couch. Lock the tripod, don't move it between takes. The hood up/down
  and the glasses are the entire transition — a whip-pan blur of 4–6 frames covers it.
- **The 21.5s silence is the most important two seconds in the video.** Everything
  before it is loud. Cutting *all* audio for one beat is what makes the turn land.
  Do not score over it. Do not shorten it.
- **Captions:** burned in, centre-frame, 3–6 words, one line at a time, bold sans with
  a hard black stroke. It has to play muted. Never let a caption survive past the line
  it belongs to.
- **Cut rhythm:** roughly one cut every 1.5–2.5s through the escalation, then *hold*
  through the turn (24.0–32.0 is only two cuts). Slowing down at the payoff is what
  signals "this is the part."
- **Music:** trending audio, ducked ~10dB under dialogue. Drop it entirely at 3.0 and
  21.5; bring it back hardest at 24.0.
- **Colour:** MANGA GUY slightly cooler/desaturated, ANIME GUY warmer. Subtle — it
  just needs to register subconsciously as two people.
- **Last frame:** hold the "Chapter 1" phone insert for 8 frames longer than feels
  right. That frame is the screenshot people paste into the comments.

## Turning this into a series

The structure is franchisable — the *joke* is "the gatekeeper gets out-gatekept." Swap
the archetype, keep the skeleton, keep the numbering (see the analysis doc, H4):

- **Pt.2 — "Mfs who only watch sub"** → dub guy turns out to voice-act professionally.
- **Pt.3 — "Mfs who say it's mid before ep 3"** → he's rewatched it four times.
- **Pt.4 — "Mfs who explain the power system"** → the quiet friend wrote the wiki page.
- **Pt.5 — "Mfs who say 'this is peak fiction' about everything"** → he's right. No reversal. Break your own format once; the comments will notice.
