# LONG-FORM 01 — "Anime Power Systems, Ranked By How Survivable They Actually Are"

**3:00 · 9:16 · fully AI · YouTube-first, cut down for Reels**

The hub video. Every "WOULD YOU SURVIVE?" Short feeds into it, and it links back out to
them. Ranked **for an ordinary person**, not for a protagonist — that reframing is the
whole idea and it's what makes the ranking arguable instead of generic.

---

Each section runs the same four beats: **character reveal → how it works → the catch →
the verdict.** Same order every time. The repetition is what makes 3 minutes feel
structured rather than long.

## Script

### HOOK · 0:00–0:20

> Every anime power system looks incredible when the main character uses it.
>
> So let's ask a different question. Not who wins. **You.** Ordinary you, no training,
> no plot armour, no destiny. Which of these actually keeps you alive?
>
> Four systems. Ranked worst to best. For you.

### #4 — CURSED ENERGY · 0:20–0:55

> Fourth. Cursed energy.
>
> It runs on negative emotion. Fear, stress, hate — all of it becomes fuel. Which
> sounds generous, until you realise the fuel is already inside you, and it's already
> leaking.
>
> Here's the catch. You can't see them. Non-sorcerers can't perceive curses at all.
> You won't be attacked — you'll be removed, and everyone will call it an accident.
>
> Verdict: this system requires emotional control under supernatural terror. Most
> people fail the entry requirement in the first ten seconds. **Fourth, and it isn't close.**

### #3 — BREATHING STYLES · 0:55–1:30

> Third. Breathing styles.
>
> No talent gate. No bloodline. Anyone can learn this — it's pure training. That alone
> puts it ahead of cursed energy, because it's the first system on this list that
> doesn't care who your parents were.
>
> The catch is what the training costs. Total concentration isn't a technique you
> switch on for a fight. It's a state you hold constantly — including while asleep. The
> strongest forms visibly wreck the body using them.
>
> Verdict: you survive the demons. You do not survive the training. The washouts are
> the lucky ones. **Third.**

### #2 — NEN · 1:30–2:08

> Second. Nen.
>
> The only system here where discipline genuinely beats talent. Six categories, and
> your type is decided by your personality — you don't pick it, you get diagnosed. And
> then you can actually *study* it. That matters enormously for an ordinary person.
>
> The catch is that the real power comes from conditions and vows. You make your
> ability stronger by binding yourself to restrictions. And the restrictions are
> enforced.
>
> Verdict: Nen doesn't kill you. Nen hands you exactly enough rope and lets you write
> the terms yourself. You survive right up until you get greedy. **Second.**

### #1 — THE SYSTEM · 2:08–2:45

> First. The System.
>
> Every other system on this list rewards what you already are. Talent, temperament,
> discipline. The System doesn't care. It levels you whether you deserve it or not.
>
> The catch is real — it scales to what it needs from you, not what you can survive,
> and it puts you in fights you should lose, because that's the mechanism. There is no
> safe grind.
>
> But it is the only system here that takes a genuinely ordinary person and makes them
> strong. Everything else filters you out. This one builds you.
>
> Verdict: you'll want to quit on day four, at the daily quest, because you thought it
> was optional. Survive that, and it's the only one that was ever going to work. **First.**

### CLOSE · 2:45–3:00

> Ranked for you. Not for the main character.
>
> Every one of these gets its own full breakdown on the channel — start at episode one.
>
> So. Would you survive? Tell me, and I'll tell you if you're wrong.

**~470 words at 3:00** — a measured pace with room for the pauses. Don't rush it.

## Editing notes

- **Chapter cards.** Burn "#4", "#3", "#2", "#1" as a full-frame card on the cut into
  each section. On a 3-minute video these are retention anchors — the viewer needs to
  see progress.
- **Verdict beat.** Same two-frame audio cut before every verdict, exactly as the
  Shorts do. Four times in this video. It becomes the sound of the channel.
- **Captions throughout.** A 3-minute video is still watched muted more often than not.
- **No intro.** Cold open on the hook, same as the Shorts. Never add a logo sting.
- **Export twice:** full 3:00 for YouTube, and cut the #1 section alone as a 40-second
  Short that links to the full video.
