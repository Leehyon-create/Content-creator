# SHORT 02 — GOJO COSPLAY TRANSITION

**~15s · 9:16 · fully AI · no dialogue**

A cosplay-transition VFX piece: **you as yourself → whip transition → Gojo → energy
build → blast → hero pose.**

**On the reference:** built from the *format* of a Gojo cosplay VFX video by
`@lucaslim3r3nce` (14.7s, TikTok-watermarked), not from their footage. The beat
structure — macro detail, whip transition, full body, energy escalation, blast, mist
hero pose — is a widely used cosplay-transition template. Every frame here is
generated from the LeehyonXP element.

**One change from the reference.** Their video is entirely in-costume. This opens on
you *out* of costume and whip-cuts into Gojo, because that is what makes it a
transition rather than a VFX reel — and it puts your real face in the video, which is
the follow-trigger the whole channel depends on.

## Why this format matters for the 1M goal

Worth stating plainly, because it may be the better bet:

| | Verdict format | Transition format |
|---|---|---|
| Language barrier | English VO required | **None** |
| Production per post | Script + VO + 8 shots | 6 shots, no writing |
| Rewatch | Low | **High — loops** |
| Shareability | Comment-driven | **Share-driven** |
| Outlier ceiling | Moderate | **High** |

Per the roadmap, 1M needs *outliers*, not consistency. Transitions travel further
because they need no translation and get re-shared rather than just watched. The
verdict series is still the follow-trigger — it gives people a reason to stay — but
transitions are the better top-of-funnel.

**Run both.** Transitions to get found, the numbered series to convert.

### Edit notes

- **The transition at 2.5s is the whole video.** Speed-ramp the last 6 frames of clip 1
  into a motion blur, cut on the fastest frame, and ramp back out of clip 2. If the
  swipe doesn't cover the cut cleanly, add a 3-frame directional blur across the join.
- **Sound design carries this**, since there's no dialogue. Whoosh on the swipe, a
  low sub-drop under the energy build, a hard impact on the blast, then near-silence
  under the mist. Beat-match the whole thing to one trending audio.
- **Flash to white** on the blast rather than cutting — it hides the resolution and
  model change between the Seedance and Kling clips.
- **Loop it.** Cut the last frame to match the first so it repeats seamlessly. Loops
  inflate watch time more than any other single edit on a 15-second video.
- **No captions, no text.** Let it play. The only text should be the caption in-app.
