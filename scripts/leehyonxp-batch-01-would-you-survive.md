## MASTER TEMPLATE — read once, then every episode is a fill-in-the-blank

The whole point of the series is that these five blocks **never change**. Same
structure, same font, same VO cadence, same outro line, every single episode. That
repetition is the follow-trigger. Resist the urge to redesign it.

| Block | Time | What it does | Changes per episode? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **COLD OPEN** | 0.0–2.0 | Title card burns in over the power system's signature visual. VO states the system and the episode number. | Visual + name only |
| **THE RULE** | 2.0–9.0 | Explain how the power system works, in one sentence, fast. Then the catch. | Yes |
| **THE PROBLEM** | 9.0–19.0 | Two escalating reasons an ordinary person is in serious trouble. One cut per reason. | Yes |
| **THE VERDICT** | 19.0–27.0 | Hard audio drop, then the ruling. A survival percentage, stated with total confidence. | Yes |
| **THE QUESTION** | 27.0–31.0 | Direct binary question. Hard cut to black mid-beat. | Never — same line every time |

**Fixed cold-open card (identical every episode):**
> **WOULD YOU SURVIVE?**
> *Ep. [N] — [POWER SYSTEM]*

**Fixed closing line (identical every episode, say it the same way):**
> "So — would you survive? Tell me, and I'll tell you if you're wrong."

That second clause is the comment engine. It gives people permission to be corrected,
which is the thing that makes them actually type.

**Fixed visual grammar across all episodes:** same bold sans caption with hard black
stroke, centre-frame, 3–6 words max, one line at a time. Same three transitions only
(hard cut, whip-pan, speed ramp). Same colour treatment. Same VO energy — measured
and certain, never hyped. Certainty is the voice of the series.

**Audio rule:** full audio cut for two frames before every verdict. Every episode. It
becomes the sound of the series.

## EP.3 — BREATHING STYLES (Demon Slayer)

**Angle — the body horror nobody mentions.** Total Concentration Breathing is treated as
training, but it is sustained physiological self-destruction.

- **RULE:** Oxygen intake pushed far past what a human body is built for. It makes you
  fast enough to fight demons.
- **PROBLEM 1:** It has to be maintained *constantly* — including while asleep. It is
  not a technique you switch on; it's a permanent state you have to hold.
- **PROBLEM 2:** The stronger forms visibly wreck the user's body. The cost isn't paid
  at the end of the fight. It's paid continuously, for the rest of a shortened life.
- **VERDICT:** "You survive the demons. You do not survive the training. Most people
  wash out in the first month — and washing out is the *good* outcome."
- **On-screen at verdict:** **"the training is the villain"**
- **Caption:** the training kills more people than the demons 💀 #demonslayer #anime
- **Pinned comment:** "constant breathing. even asleep. rethink your answer."

## EP.4 — NEN (Hunter × Hunter)

**Angle — the only system on this list that can kill you through a contract you signed
yourself.** Highest argument potential of the four; power-scaling audiences are
militant about Nen, and every correction in your comments is free distribution.

- **RULE:** Aura, six categories, and you're locked into your type by your personality.
  You don't choose it. It's diagnosed.
- **PROBLEM 1:** The initiation itself is dangerous, and a bad teacher can cripple you
  permanently before you learn anything.
- **PROBLEM 2:** The real power comes from **conditions and vows** — you make your
  ability stronger by binding yourself to restrictions. And the restrictions are
  *enforced*. People die to their own rules, not to opponents.
- **VERDICT:** "Nen doesn't kill you. Nen gives you exactly enough rope, and lets you
  write the terms yourself. You survive right up until you get greedy."
- **On-screen at verdict:** **"you write your own death"**
- **Caption:** nen is the only system where you're the one who kills you #hunterxhunter #anime #powerscaling

## S2 — "Power systems ranked by which would actually make YOU strongest" (~35s)

Verdict hook (H2). This is your **hub post** — it references the flagship series
directly, so it converts trend traffic into series viewers.

Structure: four systems, ranked worst-to-best *for an average person specifically* —
not for a protagonist. That reframing is the whole idea, and it's what makes the
ranking arguable instead of generic.

- **4th — Cursed Energy.** Requires emotional control under supernatural terror. Most
  people fail the entry requirement.
- **3rd — Breathing Styles.** Purely physical, no talent gate — but the training cost
  is permanent and it shortens your life.
- **2nd — Nen.** Enormously powerful, and the only one where discipline beats talent.
  Dangerous, but it's a system you can actually *study*.
- **1st — The System.** It levels you whether you deserve it or not. It is the only
  one on this list that makes a genuinely ordinary person strong.

**Close:** "Ranked for *you*. Not for the main character. Full breakdowns are on the
series — start at episode one." → hard cut.

## S3 — REPLY-VIDEO TEMPLATE (reusable, ~20s)

Not a one-off — a **template you reuse forever**. Every time someone disagrees with a
verdict, this is the fastest, highest-converting video you can make.

| Time | Content |
|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | Their comment on screen, read aloud verbatim. No setup, no "hey guys." |
| 2.0–5.0 | "Okay. Genuinely fair point." — concede something real first. Never open dismissive. |
| 5.0–14.0 | The counter. One specific mechanic from the source material. Be exact; vagueness gets fact-checked and fact-checks are worse than disagreement. |
| 14.0–18.0 | Revised verdict — or admit they're right. **Admitting it when they're right is worth more than winning.** People screenshot creators who concede. |
| 18.0–20.0 | "Keep them coming. Episode [N+1] is up." Hard cut. |

**Caption:** replying to @[user] — you were half right #anime #powerscaling
**Rule:** always tag the original commenter. It notifies them, they share it, and
their followers see it. This is the closest thing to a collab available at your size.
